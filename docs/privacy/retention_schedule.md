# Data Retention Schedule
| Data category | Retention and disposal |
|---|---|
| temporary masked staff-zone video | deleted after annotation and quality checking, and no later than three months after the final assessment |
| participant-code key | deleted after 26 August 2026 once valid withdrawal requests are processed |
| signed consent forms | retained securely as research records and stored separately from the research dataset |
| pseudonymised numerical dataset | retained for project analysis; public release or future reuse only with the participant's optional permission |
| recognisable assessment extracts | deleted after the relevant assessment is complete |
| customer-affected or mask-failure footage | excluded and deleted as soon as identified |
